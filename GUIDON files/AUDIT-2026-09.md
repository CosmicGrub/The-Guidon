# GUIDON audit, 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-19: what landed, what was wrong, what shipped, what is next

Scope: every change merged to `main` between 2026-09-15 and 2026-09-18 (PRs #172-#183, the "ChatGPT era"), all forks: web/PWA (the iPhone/iPad install path), standalone HTML, Windows and macOS (Tauri), Android and iOS (Capacitor), and the ESP32 handheld firmware fork. This is the technical record. The plain-language version a Soldier sees is the What's New panel inside the app.

## 1. Bottom line

- The new feature sets are good ideas and mostly sound code: Board Simulator, Team Training, PT Planner, the Cybersecurity & OPSEC curriculum, the 92A supplement deck, Study Rooms mixed decks, the memorization ladder, and a real Apple lane.
- What went wrong was almost entirely **process**, not talent: three version bumps with no release behind them, three PRs merged on red CI, new content arriving through a side door that skipped every content lint, and test suites that existed but that CI never ran.
- A 66-agent audit confirmed **54 defects (9 high, 37 medium, 8 low)**, left 32 lower-severity items unverified, and produced 52 recommendations. **v1.12.1 fixes the confirmed defects** and closes each process gap with a lint or a gate, so the same mistake fails a build next time instead of reaching a Soldier.
- By your decision, **v1.12.1 supersedes v1.11.0 and v1.12.0**, which were prepared but never released.

## 3. What the audit found

The nine high-severity findings:

1. **Study Rooms mixed decks did nothing from the real UI.** The "Host a room" button bypassed the add-on shim, so a multi-category selection was silently ignored; worse, the shim replaced `G.store.boardQuestions` globally and shrank the whole app's bank during and after a mixed room.
2. **Both in-app download buttons returned 404.** `#/share` and the install QR point at fixed-name files that only v1.9.0 carried; v1.10.0 became Latest without them.
3. **The automated release path dropped the fixed-name files and the downloads table** every time, so the 404 would have recurred on every release.
4. **Three versions were bumped and none was released**, while CHANGELOG, ROADMAP and README described them as shipped.
5. **The Android release job could never succeed**: the repository has no Actions secrets (on purpose), and the job had no honest fallback.
6. **`main` was red for four consecutive commits**; both "release" PRs were merged with failing checks.
7. **PR #179 merged with a JavaScript syntax error** in a content module; the test matrix was skipped and nobody noticed.
8. **Nothing stops a red merge**: `main` has no required status checks, and GitHub Pages deployed every push regardless of CI.
9. **New runtime cards left CI red** (the consistency count expected 1,265; the bank was 1,274).

The medium and low findings cluster into five themes:

- **Content truth.** A weapons card taught a "fourth safety rule" TC 3-22.9 does not contain; an NCO Creed card attributed a line the Creed does not contain; ~200 paraphrased cards rendered under a "verbatim doctrine" heading; superseded paragraph cites; pack categories that duplicated a seed subject under another name (one subject, two chips, two heatmap rows).
- **Rights.** The complete text of a modern, named-author unit song shipped with "user-supplied" as its only provenance - the same problem that held the cadences back.
- **The OPSEC guard damaged what it screened.** Its SSN pattern deleted Army citations from MOI imports; its block words were case-insensitive English, so ordinary study text (including GUIDON's own) tripped it while real marking syntax like `(S//NF)` passed; the curriculum module overwrote existing dictionary definitions (AO lost "area of operations").
- **Feature logic.** PT Planner's `var`-in-a-loop saved the wrong day, reminders never expired, and a template change wiped per-date edits without asking. Board Simulator marked a phase complete when you pressed Exit and dead-ended for E1-E3. Team Training dead-ended outside E4-E6. The memorization ladder What's New announced had no screen.
- **Tests that guarded nothing.** Five suites had script entries but were in no CI path; several "tests" grepped source strings instead of driving the feature; the iOS WebKit check carried `continue-on-error`, so it printed 19 defects and stayed green for weeks; the iOS Simulator retry wrapper lost its deadline in a "release:" commit and nothing ran it.

## 4. What v1.12.1 changes

**Fixed (91 individual fixes across nine parallel branches, then integrated):**

- *Rights:* the song text is removed in full. Four sourced heritage cards remain. Recitation Drill gains **My unit** - a Soldier pastes their own unit song, creed or motto; it is stored only on the device, and text carrying a classification marking is refused. A rights gate fails the build if bundled full text lacks `{author, firstPublished, basis, evidence}`.
- *Content:* contradicted and mis-cited cards reconciled against current publications; paraphrased cards are labeled as study-guide answers; six duplicate categories merged. 1,274 -> 1,230 cards, 357 doctrine entries, 195 scenarios.
- *Features:* Study Rooms mixed decks rebuilt inside `studygroup.js` (up to 12 subjects, lossless on the wire, older seats told plainly); OPSEC guard rebuilt findings-only (`screen()` reports, never rewrites; only real marking syntax is a hard stop); PT Planner, Board Simulator and Team Training logic fixes; the Recall ladder is a real Recitation mode; clipped focus rings and off-screen deep-linked filter chips fixed; a button that overflowed a folded phone on the Cyber/OPSEC page fixed.
- *Apple:* `NSFaceIDUsageDescription` added (Face ID would have crashed the native iOS build); the Simulator retry wrapper restored and tested; the WebKit pre-flight is a real gate again, as a ratchet - 18 known defects tolerated by name, anything new fails, anything fixed must leave the list.

**New scaffolding (so the same class of mistake fails a build):**

| Gap that bit us | What now catches it |
|---|---|
| Runtime content packs skipped every content lint | `tools/assemble-bank.mjs` evaluates the seed plus every numbered pack headlessly; `tools/lint-content-packs.mjs` (rules p1-p9, in `lint:patterns`) lints the assembled bank; consistency counts and the ESP32 exporter read the same bank |
| Pillar tags hand-set per pack, drifting from the map | `98-content-pack-finalize.js` tags from the one definition (`tools/pillar-map.mjs`, injected by the build); lint fails on any disagreement |
| Three writers of the study-room bank fingerprint | One, stamped after the last pack |
| A suite exists but CI never runs it | `lint-ci-matrix` rule (d): every `tools/test-*.mjs` is in the matrix or has a written reason (201 suites / 51 chunks) |
| Version bumped in some files, not others (iOS was missed) | `tools/bump-version.mjs` + `tools/lint-release-state.mjs` |
| A tag cut from a red commit; Pages deploying a red commit | `release-cut.yml` and `pages.yml` both wait for green CI on that exact commit |
| Release with missing fixed-name files | Asset jobs always upload them; finalize marks Latest only when the release is complete |
| Android unsignable in CI | Honest fallback: signed on the owner's machine, attached, `finalize_only` run (`docs/release-runbook.md`) |
| "New" badges stuck on a three-release-old wave | One wave, declared once (`NEW_WAVE`), lint-held; tests read the live wave |

Two integration seams surfaced only when the nine branches met, and are the argument for the module contract in section 6: the My unit feature called a guard function another branch had removed and, because the call was wrapped in `typeof ... === "function"`, it *silently skipped the marking refusal*; and three suites hard-coded a deck size that a category merge changed.

## 5. Decisions that are yours

**Repository settings (I cannot and should not change these):**

1. Require the **`CI green`** status check on `main`. The workflows now refuse to tag or deploy a red commit, but only this setting stops a red *merge*.
2. Protect **`refs/tags/v*`** from update and deletion, so a version tag is permanent by rule rather than habit.
3. Android signing stays on your machine (current, safest). The alternative is adding the four `ANDROID_*` secrets; the same job then signs in CI with no workflow change. I did not create or handle any secret.

**Rights and content:**

4. The unit song stays out unless you document a basis (official-duty Government work, pre-1978 publication without notice, or written permission). The gate will then accept it with a `rights{}` record.
5. The lyric is still in **git history** (merge `59e1d66`). Rewriting history is your call; I did not touch it.
6. Three older Creeds entries (`creed-night-stalker`, `creed-combat-medic`, `creed-cadet`) carry full text with no rights record. They cite official Army bodies and predate the rule, so the gate lists them by id instead of failing. Someone should research and record a basis for each.
7. Unit-heritage cards (1st Cavalry Division) are visible to every Soldier. Making them opt-in needs a "my unit" profile setting that does not exist yet.
8. Card-back heading on ~195 paraphrased cards: "Study-guide answer (not a word-for-word quote)". New user-facing copy - change the wording if you prefer.
9. Schedule the remaining AR 710-2 -> AR 710-4 pass (6 cards, 3 doctrine entries, 1 scenario reference). DA Form 7923's edition date was removed rather than guessed.
10. Still open from before: the cadences content policy, and the View Transitions prototype.

**Product behaviour (each implemented as the safest reversible option):**

11. MOS decks: a card with an MOS list is hidden only when the profile names a *different* MOS. Decide on an opt-in to study another MOS, and whether the 40 92A cards should count toward the Maintenance & Supply pillar or a separate "My MOS" row.
12. OPSEC guard severity: an SSN, a labelled DoD ID, a "this document is SECRET" sentence, or a future date + place + unit activity *pause* the import and let the Soldier continue; only real marking syntax is a hard stop. A bare line reading exactly `SECRET` / `CUI` is treated as a banner.
13. PT Planner: the intensity dial was removed rather than faked (wiring it needs a mapping decision against the PRT Standalone/Combined toggle). Plan sharing is a manual file hand-off; decide whether Study Rooms should carry plans later. Hand-made "pt" reminders also expire after their date.
14. Board Simulator: E7-E9 borrow the E4-E6 leadership problem because the seed has none of their own; the reporting scenario keeps its E4-E6 tag in the Train catalog.
15. Guest and Kiosk sessions still write the new feature keys even though onboarding says nothing is saved. That is app-wide and should be decided once in `G.db`, not per module.
16. Team Training results land in the device owner's personal attempt record (`mode: "collective"`). Recommended: keep group results out of the personal record.

**Release and platform:**

17. Mac: only some releases carry a DMG and there is no fixed-name Mac file, so `#/share` links to the releases list. If every release will carry a DMG, the Mac button can become a direct download. Without an Apple Developer ID the first-open warning stays; `docs/mac-first-launch.md` is the mitigation.
18. iOS build number: +1 per version (now 5). The alternative is Android's formula (11201); `--ios-build <n>` supports either.
19. The 18 tolerated WebKit defects (inputs under 16px that make iOS zoom on focus; tap targets under 44px; one unbounded `75vh`): schedule fixing them. Each fix deletes its baseline line in the same change.
20. Should untagged builds stamp themselves `x.y.z-dev+sha` so the hosted web app never identifies as a release that was never cut? Not done: it changes the version string every user sees.

## 6. Recommendations: scaffolding for the new feature sets

Ordered by value for effort. The theme: the new features arrived as *patches on the app*; they should become *registered parts of it*.

### Now (small, each lands green on its own)

- **A. A module manifest with named extension points** (audit R9/R39/R50). Today load order is alphabetical filenames, and modules add themselves by wrapping `G.board.render`, `G.drills.render`, `G.views.prt` and `G.engine.run`. Replace that with one manifest - `{ id, requires, provides, routes, storageKeys, contentPack }` per module - and named hooks (`G.ext.on("drills:menu", ...)`). Nav, the Guided Tour, the backup validators and the lints all read the same list. Add one lint: a `typeof G.x.y === "function"` guard must name an API that some module's `provides` declares, which turns the silent-skip seam from section 4 into a build failure.
- **B. Put the new storage keys under the storage contract** (R42). `board:sim:v1`, `team:training:v1`, `prt:plan:v1`, `pt:history:v1`, `recall-ladder:*` and `guidon:recite:own:v1` have no backup validators. Register them, and settle the Guest/Kiosk write question (decision 15) in the same change.
- **C. Shared test helpers plus a lint ratchet on swallowed waits** (R38). The flake signatures in this repo are nearly all click-race and wait-timing; a `clickWhenStable()` / `waitForRoute()` pair, adopted suite by suite under a ratchet, is cheaper than the next three flake hunts. Add "derive counts from the live bank" to the new-suite scaffold: three suites broke this week on a literal `17`.
- **D. Generated content floors** (R35). Replace hand-bumped literals (`FLOORS = { board: 1230, ... }`) with a committed manifest the build regenerates and the lint ratchets: counts may rise freely, and fall only with an explicit `--allow-shrink` note in the same commit.

### Next (the structural ones)

- **E. Build-time content packs behind one API** (R25/R44/R48/R52) - the single highest-value change. Keep authoring packs as separate files, but merge them into the seed at *build* time through `G.contentPack.define({ id, version, cards, doctrine, scenarios, terms, rights })`, instead of each pack pushing into `window.GUIDON_SEED` at load. Everything that reads the seed - lints, counts, the ESP32 exporter, the standalone build's hash, search - then sees one bank by construction, and `assemble-bank.mjs` becomes the build step rather than a parallel re-implementation of it. The audit laid out a five-step migration that keeps card ids and study history intact and lands green at each step.
- **F. A deck authoring schema that enforces citation honesty** (R21/R47). Structured citations - `{ pub, edition, para, quoteKind: "verbatim" | "paraphrase" }` - instead of free-text `source` strings. "Verbatim" headings, regulation chips, the superseded-publication check (AR 710-2 -> 710-4) and the rights gate all become mechanical. The ~200 relabeled cards are the proof of need.
- **G. MOS decks as a first-class opt-in lane** (R22). 92A is the first of many. A lane has its own picker entry and its own Readiness row, is gated by profile MOS with an explicit "study another MOS" opt-in, and does not inflate the six promotion-board pillars. Resolves decision 11.
- **H. An engine completion contract; Collective mode in the core** (R45/R46). The Board Simulator infers "phase done" from side effects, and Collective Decision monkeypatches `G.engine.run` with a fifth argument. Give `G.engine.run()` a real result (`{ finished, outcome, score, path }`), make "collective" a render mode of the engine, and build the simulator on a phase registry. Run history and printable after-action notes then fall out naturally.
- **I. One PRT session model** (R40). The PT Planner, `#/prt` and `#/drills` each describe a PT session differently. One model in the seed, consumed by all three, is what lets the intensity dial (decision 13) come back honestly.

### Later (worth planning, not urgent)

- **J. Data-driven, release-aware What's New** (R18): entries generated from the same manifest as the CHANGELOG, with the lint already in place checking the plain-language rules.
- **K. The legal package as a verified artifact** (R26): every factual sentence in `GUIDON_COMMAND_LEGAL_PACKAGE.md` maps to a named test, and the document is stamped with the version and commit it was verified against.
- **L. Release orchestration** (R10/R13/R19): keep the hardened two-stage design (a draft release creates no tag, and every asset job checks out the tag). Add a macOS launch proof and notarization-readiness, a fixed-name Mac file, and the `-dev+sha` stamp if you approve decision 20.
- **M. Wider scope the new features open up:** Study Rooms carrying PT plans and Team Training sessions (one hand-off model instead of three); per-unit content packs through the same `contentPack` API, with My unit as the on-device half; Board Simulator run history feeding the Readiness rollup; a Leader view of Team Training results kept out of any one Soldier's record; and the ESP32 handheld consuming lanes (an MOS deck on the device) once E and G exist.

## 7. How this was verified

- 66-agent audit; every confirmed finding was reproduced by a second, adversarial agent before it counted. 32 lower-severity items were *not* sent to a verifier and are listed as unverified, not as fact.
- Nine fix branches, each with a regression assertion that fails on the old code, then one integration branch.
- `npm run lint:patterns`: 12 gates. `npm run verify`: 34 checks across 9 viewports. 201 browser suites locally and in CI (51 chunks), plus the iOS WebKit gate, the iOS Simulator build, the Desktop build, and the Android signing-continuity audit.
- Android artifacts: signer certificate, `versionName` and `versionCode` read back from the built files with `apksigner`, `keytool` and `aapt2`.
